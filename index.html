02 / BUT A FEW THINGS HAVE MY ATTENTION

Not every issue is a ticket.
Not every ticket is simple.

I’m the link between clients who need answers and the teams who can make them happen. I handle L1 support across accounts receivable and accounts payable, investigate escalations, document reproducible bugs, and keep follow-through visible until the issue is resolved.

That means asking the right questions, checking the evidence, setting a clear priority, and making sure the next person has what they need to act.

CLIENTS ↔ OPERATIONS ↔ ENGINEERING
03 / SOMEHOW, I KEEP ENDING UP...

taking ownership.

When an issue needs more than a first-line answer, I move into investigation mode: checking Firestore or MongoDB, collecting evidence, reproducing the issue in a testing tenant, and raising a structured bug ticket in Notion when needed.

Then I stay with it. I assign the right engineering owner, tag the sprint, help rank work from high to low priority, and flag urgent fixes that may need to be cherry-picked instead of waiting for a regular release.

notice it→investigate it→reproduce it→assign it→follow through↻
